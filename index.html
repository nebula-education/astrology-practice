Track: Relationships
▸ Foundation — Why this track exists

Astrology for relationships isn't fortune-telling. It's pattern-reading.

People rarely arrive with the question, "is this person my soulmate?" More often they ask: what kind of partner do I attract? How do I express love? Why do these patterns repeat? The chart doesn't predict timing or outcomes — it reveals the emotional blueprint underneath.

For a mini reading, four placements do almost all the work. We're going to read them in sequence, then synthesize.

▸ Step 1 of 4

Sun & Moon — your emotional baseline

The Sun is conscious direction — how someone acts and expresses will. The Moon is emotional instinct — what they need to feel safe. Read together, they tell you the basic push-and-pull between outer and inner worlds. Do they support each other, or pull in different directions?

✶
Click the image on the left to reveal the reading.

Example — Aries Sun × Cancer Moon

This combination mixes fierce independence with deep protectiveness. The Aries Sun wants to lead, initiate, and stay sovereign. The Cancer Moon needs closeness, emotional safety, and a sense of home. Both are fiercely loyal — but they pull in different directions.

In relationships, this person can love hard and withdraw fast. They crave intimacy but feel suffocated when controlled. Recognising the tension is the work: it's not a flaw to fix, it's a rhythm to balance — initiative plus empathy, not initiative versus empathy.

Quick rule: if Sun and Moon harmonize (same element or compatible), expect emotional steadiness. If they clash, expect lifelong learning around self-expression and sensitivity — and partners who keep mirroring that tension back.

Aries Sun Cancer Moon Independence + closeness
★ Try it on your own chart

Pull up your chart and find the Sun and Moon glyphs (☉ and ☾). Note their signs.

  • Are they in compatible elements (Fire/Air or Earth/Water), or do they clash?
  • Which one feels more dominant in how you actually behave day-to-day?
  • Write one sentence: "I want to ___, but I need to feel ___."

▸ Step 2 of 4

Venus — your love language

Venus shows what you find beautiful and how you give affection. Note its sign and house. Air-sign Venus loves through words; Earth-sign Venus loves through actions and consistency. Aspect Venus to Mars for chemistry, to Saturn for commitment themes.

✶
Click the image on the left to reveal the reading.

Example — Venus in Taurus vs. Venus in Gemini

Venus in Taurus needs physical and emotional stability. Loyalty, shared comfort, predictability. Loves through touch, food, returning to the same restaurant. The shadow: resistance to change. They may stay in a relationship for security rather than growth. The lesson is that real comfort comes from trust, not control.

Venus in Gemini values communication, curiosity, and mental connection. They fall in love through conversation and need ongoing intellectual stimulation to stay engaged. The shadow: restlessness, surface-level flirting. The lesson is that depth comes from staying with one mind long enough to actually finish a thought together.

How to use this: match Venus's element to the way affection is expressed. If you're reading for someone whose Venus element doesn't match their partner's, name it — that's where most "they don't show love right" complaints come from.

Venus = comfort filter Sign + house = how + where Aspect to Mars / Saturn
★ Try it on your own chart

Find Venus (♀) on your wheel. Note its sign and the house number it sits in.

  • Sign tells you how you love — translate it into a sentence.
  • House tells you where love shows up most (1H = identity, 7H = partnership, 10H = public life, etc.).
  • Look at lines drawn from Venus — those aspects colour the picture further.

▸ Step 3 of 4

Ascendant — the style others meet first

The Ascendant (Rising sign) describes how you approach love and handle conflict. Libra Rising seeks agreement; Aries Rising dives in fearlessly; Capricorn Rising prefers boundaries and gradual trust. The ruling planet of the Ascendant shows how those instincts get projected outward.

✶
Click the image on the left to reveal the reading.

Example — Libra Rising vs. Capricorn Rising

Libra Rising, ruled by Venus, brings grace, charm, and diplomacy into first impressions. They adapt easily to others' moods — sometimes too easily, leaning on external validation to feel okay. The growth path: learning to decide from inner balance rather than constant comparison. When integrated, Libra Rising shows the art of peaceful connection without self-loss.

Capricorn Rising, ruled by Saturn, approaches relationships cautiously and seriously. They take time to open up and value stability, reliability, and long-term potential. They appear reserved at first and prefer to build trust gradually — showing commitment through consistency rather than emotional intensity. Read the slow start as care, not coldness.

Pro move: always check the Ascendant ruler. Where it sits (its house) often tells you where this person actually directs their love energy, regardless of what their words say.

Ascendant = style Ruler = projection First impression filter
★ Try it on your own chart

Find your Ascendant — it's the sign on the left horizon line of the wheel (the 9-o'clock position).

  • What's the first impression people typically have of you? Does it match?
  • Find the planet that rules your Ascendant sign and note where it sits.
  • Compare: does your Sun-Moon story match this outer style, or contradict it?

▸ Step 4 of 4 — Synthesis

Now assemble the pattern.

Compare your Sun-Moon, Venus, and Ascendant side by side. Repetition is the message: if several of them emphasize Fire, relationships thrive on activity and shared goals. If Water predominates, emotional bonding is essential. Conflicts between elements explain attraction and friction.

Fire-heavy

Action, novelty, and shared goals. Relationships need momentum or they fizzle.

Air-heavy

Conversation, ideas, intellectual chemistry. Silence reads as distance.

Water-heavy

Emotional bonding, intuition, depth. Logic-only relationships feel hollow.

Earth-heavy

Loyalty, consistency, real-world reliability. Big gestures matter less than showing up.

★ Reading rule

Astrology works by repetition. Patterns speak louder than single aspects. Write down each repeating theme — fire that keeps showing up, water that keeps showing up, the same house mentioned twice. That repetition is the main lesson the chart offers about love.

▸ Foundation — Why this track exists

Astrology for career is about direction, not job titles.

Career questions usually arrive disguised as confusion: "I don't know what I'm good at." "Why do I keep feeling stuck?" "What suits me?" The chart won't hand back a job title — it points at strengths, direction, and the environment that lets a person actually shine.

For a mini reading on career, three placements do most of the lifting — Sun & Moon, the Midheaven, and the Ascendant. Same algorithm as the Relationships track, with Venus swapped for the MC.

▸ Step 1 of 4

Sun & Moon — talents & satisfaction

The Sun shows where you want to shine — what you're naturally good at and where you express your will. The Moon shows what feels emotionally fulfilling and safe. In a career reading, ask: where does this person want to lead, and what kind of work makes them feel okay at the end of the day?

✶
Click the image on the left to reveal the reading.

Example — Sun + Moon in Capricorn, 4th house

Both luminaries sitting in Capricorn in the 4th house describe someone who is disciplined, responsible, and focused on building stability. Their talents show up in structured work, long-term planning, and creating something secure that lasts.

The 4th-house emphasis makes home, family, or a controlled private environment central — they may do their best work from a quiet, ordered base rather than an open-plan office. This person feels fulfilled when they can build something stable and meaningful over time, not when they're chasing fast wins.

Read this combo as a signal: don't push them into volatile, high-novelty fields. Their growth happens through depth and patience, not through pivots.

Capricorn × 4H Discipline + privacy Long-horizon work
★ Try it on your own chart

Find your Sun and Moon. Note both signs and both houses.

  • Sign = flavor of the talent. House = arena where it expresses.
  • If they're in the same sign or same house, that's a strong, focused talent line — name it concretely.
  • If they conflict, write the tension as a sentence: "I want to lead in ___, but I feel safe in ___."

▸ Step 2 of 4

Midheaven — your career direction

The Midheaven (MC) sits at the very top of the chart — the 12-o'clock point. It represents the overall direction of your career, the type of environment you're drawn to, and how you want to be seen professionally. It's the public face of your ambition.

✶
Click the image on the left to reveal the reading.

Example — Midheaven in Aries

An Aries Midheaven points to a career path that requires initiative, independence, and leadership. This person thrives in dynamic environments where they can take action, start projects, and make quick decisions.

They feel restricted in passive roles — committee work, slow consensus-building, anything where they have to wait their turn. They need autonomy to feel fulfilled, and they're often happiest as the first person doing a thing rather than the tenth person doing it well.

Practical translation: founder roles, lead-this-launch energy, sports, emergency professions, anything with "first" or "head of" in the title. Read the MC as the vector the career wants to point in — not the specific job.

Aries MC Initiative Lead, don't follow
★ Try it on your own chart

Find your Midheaven — it's the cusp of the 10th house, sitting at the top of the wheel.

  • What's the sign? Translate it into one direction-word: lead, build, communicate, nurture, perform, serve, balance, transform…
  • Where does the MC's ruling planet sit? That house describes where the ambition actually plays out.
  • Does your current job match the direction? If not — what's the smallest shift toward the MC vector you could make this quarter?

▸ Step 3 of 4

Ascendant — skills & self-expression

The Ascendant shows how you act in real situations — your behaviour, your approach to work, and the skills you naturally rely on. It's also how others perceive you the moment you walk into a meeting. In a career reading, treat the Ascendant as the method someone uses to actually do the work.

✶
Click the image on the left to reveal the reading.

Example — Gemini Rising

Gemini Rising expresses through intellect, communication, and adaptability. This person relies on quick thinking and the ability to process and share information rapidly.

They thrive in areas like media, sales, writing, teaching, marketing — anything connected to communication and information flow. Their strength is versatility, networking, and connecting ideas to ideas, and people to people.

The shadow: scattering. Ten browser tabs, three side-projects, no finished one. The growth path is which conversation to commit to — narrowing, not broadening.

Reading rule: match the Ascendant's element to the daily texture of the work. Air → people, words, ideas. Earth → tangible outputs. Fire → motion, novelty. Water → people's emotional states.

Gemini Rising Versatility Communication-driven
★ Try it on your own chart

Look at your Ascendant sign and ask:

  • What's the texture of how you work? Quick + verbal? Slow + tangible? Big-picture + restless?
  • Does your current role match that texture, or does it fight it?
  • If your MC is the destination, your Ascendant is the vehicle — are they compatible, or are you driving a tractor toward a sprint finish?
4
▸ Step 4 of 4 — Synthesis

Combine all three for the full picture.

Always read the layers together. Sun + Moon = what you're good at and what brings satisfaction. Midheaven = the direction and the kind of environment. Ascendant = how you act and succeed in real life. Patterns matter more than isolated meanings.

Sun + Moon

Talents and satisfaction. Where you want to shine, and what makes work feel meaningful.

Midheaven

Direction and environment. The vector the career wants to point in, regardless of job title.

Ascendant

Method and texture. How you actually do the work — the vehicle that gets you to the MC.

Together

The full reading: a person who builds this kind of thing, in this kind of environment, using this kind of approach.

★ Reading rule

Focus on patterns, not isolated placements. If three of the four placements emphasize the same element, that's the headline. If they conflict, that conflict is the story — and the person's career growth usually involves negotiating it consciously rather than picking a side.
